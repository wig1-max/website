FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about company registration in Panchkula.

The right business structure depends on your specific needs. If you are a solo entrepreneur with minimal compliance requirements, a Sole Proprietorship is the simplest and most affordable option. If you have a partner, a Partnership Firm or LLP offers flexibility with limited liability. For startups seeking funding or planning to scale, a Private Limited Company is the most preferred choice due to limited liability and investor confidence. If you want to run a non-profit, a Section 8 Company is ideal. During your free consultation, we assess your goals, number of partners, funding plans, and liability preferences to recommend the best fit.

The registration timeline depends on the type of entity. A Sole Proprietorship can be set up in 3-5 working days. Partnership Firm registration typically takes 5-7 working days. One Person Company and Private Limited Company registration takes 7-15 working days, depending on name availability and MCA processing times. LLP registration usually takes 10-15 working days. Section 8 Company (NGO) registration can take 30-45 days due to the additional license requirement from the Central Government. We handle the entire process end-to-end and keep you updated at every step.

Yes, all government fees are included in our quoted prices. This covers stamp duty, MCA filing fees, DSC costs, DIN fees, name reservation charges, and ROC filing fees. There are no hidden charges or surprise costs. The price you see on our website is the total price you pay. We believe in complete transparency, and we provide a detailed breakdown of all costs before you commit.

Yes, a registered office address is required for all types of company registrations in India. This address will be used for all official correspondence from the Registrar of Companies (ROC) and other government authorities. The registered office can be a commercial space, a co-working address, or even a residential address (for certain entity types). You will need to provide proof of the address such as a rent agreement or utility bill. If you do not have an office yet, we can guide you on the best options available in Panchkula and the Tricity area.

After registration, the ongoing compliance requirements depend on your business structure. For a Private Limited Company, you need to file annual returns (AOC-4 and MGT-7) with the ROC, hold board meetings and AGMs, maintain statutory registers, and file income tax returns. GST filing is required if your turnover exceeds the threshold or if you have voluntarily registered. For LLPs, you need to file Form 8 (Statement of Accounts) and Form 11 (Annual Return) annually. Sole proprietorships and partnerships have simpler compliance — mainly ITR filing and GST returns. Our Private Limited Company package includes first-year compliance support for free, and we offer ongoing annual compliance packages for all entity types.
