Why Audits Matter for Your Business

An audit is more than a regulatory checkbox. It is a comprehensive review of your financial records, internal controls, and business processes that provides stakeholders, including investors, banks, regulators, and business partners, with confidence in the accuracy and reliability of your financial statements. For businesses operating in Panchkula and the Chandigarh Tricity area, timely and thorough audits are essential for maintaining compliance with Indian laws and building long-term credibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our audit services.

A statutory audit is mandatory for all companies registered under the Companies Act 2013, regardless of their turnover or size. This includes private limited companies, public limited companies, one person companies (OPCs), and Section 8 companies. The audit must be conducted by a qualified Chartered Accountant and the audited financial statements must be filed with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) annually. At Omzato, we work closely with licensed CAs to ensure your statutory audit meets every legal requirement.

Section 44AB of the Income Tax Act mandates a tax audit for businesses whose annual turnover exceeds Rs 1 crore (Rs 10 crore if 95% or more of transactions are digital) and for professionals whose gross receipts exceed Rs 50 lakh in a financial year. The tax audit involves a detailed examination of books of accounts and results in the preparation of Form 3CD, which is filed along with the income tax return. The due date for tax audit is typically 30th September of the assessment year. We handle the entire process from books review to Form 3CD preparation and ITR filing support.

A typical audit takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on the size and complexity of the business. Factors that affect the timeline include the volume of transactions, the number of branches or locations, the state of existing books of accounts, and the type of audit being conducted. We work efficiently to minimise disruption to your business operations and provide regular progress updates via WhatsApp throughout the process.
