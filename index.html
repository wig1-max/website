Registering a company through Omzato is a simple 4-step process:

  1. Free Consultation — We understand your business needs and recommend the right structure (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, etc.).
  2. Document Collection — We guide you on the required documents and handle Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) and Director Identification Numbers (DIN).
  3. Filing & Registration — We draft the MOA/AOA, file via SPICe+ with the ROC, and handle PAN/TAN registration.
  4. Post-Registration Setup — We assist with bank account opening, GST registration, and first-year compliance.

Private Limited Company registration starts from ₹14,999 including all government fees.
